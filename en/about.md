# About this blog

This site publishes research notes, method descriptions, and verified aggregate results from EvidencePatch.

The project asks how traceable, verifiable, budget-aware evidence supplements can respond to reviewer concerns while the paper’s core content remains frozen—and how to distinguish easier evidence access from the resolution of a real research flaw.

Posts state their data scope, evaluation method, and limitations. Paper full text, per-paper identifiers, and information that could reveal individual papers are not published before reuse-rights review.
